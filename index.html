Washington → Canada

A quiz for the drive, built for Ernel and Angela.

Ernel and Angela, this is your Washington-to-Canada road-trip trivia night, and Easton or Achilles can jump in too. Choose two, three, or four players, pick Adult or Family, then choose alternating turns or a shared buzz-in pool, and decide whether to play 20, 30, or 50 questions.

The questions are tougher now, running from moderately hard to genuinely hard, and harder ones are worth more points; tap a letter, or hit the mic and say the answer (or say A, B, C, or D). After each guess you'll see the right answer and a short explanation, then move on.

The game remembers questions you've already seen and shuffles a fresh set each time; at the end you can rematch or start a new game.
